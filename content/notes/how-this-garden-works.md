Each note has a `status` in its frontmatter:

- **seedling**: a rough first thought, probably incomplete or wrong
- **budding**: taking shape, but still being reworked
- **evergreen**: well developed and maintained

Notes are plain Markdown files, so they work in any editor. Links use the `[[wiki link]]` style, and every page shows its backlinks, so connections appear automatically. See [[linking-notes]].
